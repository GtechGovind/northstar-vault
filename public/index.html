Privacy center

Your vault, your control.

Google Sign-In verifies your identity. Every private request is checked on the server, and your reflections stay under your own account.

Account-level isolation.
Firebase tokens are verified for each private API request. Direct browser database writes are denied.

Server-side AI access.
Gemini credentials never reach your browser. Northstar does not reuse entries to train its own models.

Deletion with safeguards.
Late AI replies cannot restore erased entries. Erasure keeps only an opaque anti-replay marker, not journal content. It does not remove your Google/Firebase account or cloud-provider backups.
